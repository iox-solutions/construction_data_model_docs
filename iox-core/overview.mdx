## What is IOX Core?

IOX Core is the data backbone of the IOX Platform. It defines and owns the authoritative record for every concept that is shared across the platform: the projects being managed, the contracts in place, the organisations involved, the people using the system, the documents being exchanged, the costs being tracked.

Every module in the IOX Platform — ParametriX today, others in the future — is built on top of IOX Core. Modules do not maintain their own copies of shared data. They reference IOX Core directly.

This means there is one version of a project, one version of a contract, one version of a user. No reconciliation. No drift. No conflicts between what one module thinks and what another believes.

## Current version

IOX Core is currently at **v1.13**, with all 12 functional segments documented. 10 segments are fully complete. 2 segments (QA and Workflow & Templates) are partially documented as stubs, pending engineering clarification on the full specification.

The model covers approximately **65 entities** and **140 relationships**, governed by **29 architectural decisions**.

## How IOX Core is governed

IOX Core is version-controlled. Every change — every new entity, every new attribute, every architectural decision — goes through the standard approval workflow and is recorded in the IOX Core Changelog.

The Changelog is the single source of truth for the history of the model. It records what changed, when, who approved it, and whether the change was backward compatible.
